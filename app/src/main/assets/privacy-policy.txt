Just Run Privacy Policy
Effective date: June 9, 2026

Just Run is a local-first running, fitness, and daily activity app. It is designed to work without a Just Run server account, without developer-operated cloud storage, and without advertising or tracking SDKs.

Health and medical disclaimer

Just Run is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, injury, or medical condition. Just Run is intended for general fitness and personal activity tracking only.

Fitness, calorie, heart-rate, pace, cadence, elevation, and activity values shown by the app are estimates and may be inaccurate or unavailable depending on your device, sensors, permissions, environment, and settings. Do not rely on Just Run for medical decisions, emergency use, safety-critical decisions, or professional training advice.

Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or before starting or changing an exercise program if you have health concerns.

Summary

Just Run does not sell your data.
Just Run does not send your activity data to a Just Run server.
Just Run does not create a developer-operated account for you.
Just Run stores your run and activity data on your own devices.
Just Run requests only the permissions needed for the features you use.
You can export or share data yourself, such as by exporting GPX files or sending diagnostic logs.

Data the app may access or create

Depending on the features you enable, Just Run may access, create, or store:

- Run activity data, such as elapsed time, distance, pace, lap splits, calories, cadence, elevation, and run history.
- Precise location data, if GPS tracking is enabled, including route points used for distance, maps, elevation, GPX import/export, and run summaries.
- Health and fitness sensor data, such as heart rate, step count, cadence, and calorie estimates, when supported by your phone or Wear OS watch and enabled by you.
- Profile settings you enter, such as height, weight, age, unit preference, activity goals, lap settings, voice cue settings, and watch settings.
- Imported or exported files, such as GPX files that you choose to import into or export from the app.
- Diagnostic logs and crash details generated by the app, such as app activity breadcrumbs, device/app version details, and stack traces.

Just Run does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, messages, payment information, government identifiers, or account credentials.

How the app uses data

Just Run uses this data to provide app features, including:

- Recording and summarizing runs.
- Showing live run stats on the phone and Wear OS watch.
- Estimating pace, distance, calories, cadence, elevation, and daily activity progress.
- Producing voice cues during runs.
- Updating Android home screen widgets and Wear OS complications.
- Importing and exporting GPX files when you choose to do so.
- Helping diagnose crashes or bugs if you choose to retrieve and share diagnostic logs.

Just Run does not use health, fitness, or location data for advertising, profiling, sale, credit decisions, insurance decisions, employment decisions, or any purpose unrelated to the app's fitness and troubleshooting features.

Where data is stored

Just Run stores app data locally on your phone and, for Wear OS features, locally on your watch. Phone and watch data may sync with each other through Android/Wear OS device communication so that the phone, watch app, widgets, and complications can show consistent activity information.

Just Run does not operate a server that receives or stores your activity data.

Network access

Just Run may open user-selected links, such as the project repository, issue tracker, or this privacy policy, in a browser or another app. Just Run does not use network access to upload your run, health, location, or diagnostic data to a Just Run server.

Sharing and disclosure

Just Run does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with the developer or third parties. The developer does not receive your run, health, location, diagnostic, or profile data unless you choose to send it.

Data may leave your device only when you choose to take an action outside the app's local storage, such as:

- Exporting a GPX file.
- Sharing an exported file.
- Backing up your device using Android or another backup provider.
- Sending diagnostic logs or crash information to someone for troubleshooting.
- Opening a link in another app or browser.

If you use third-party services, device backups, file sharing apps, cloud drives, operating system features, or GitHub, their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Permissions

Just Run may request permissions for the following purposes:

- Location: to record outdoor route points, calculate run distance and pace, show maps or route summaries, and create GPX data.
- Physical activity and sensors: to read steps, cadence, heart rate, and related fitness sensor values when supported by your device.
- Notifications and foreground service access: to keep active run tracking visible and controllable while a run is in progress.
- Nearby device or Wear OS communication features, where applicable: to sync live run status, controls, and daily activity values between your phone and watch.
- File access or document picker access, where applicable: to import or export GPX files that you choose.

Permissions that are not needed for Just Run's fitness features should not be requested. You can deny or revoke permissions in Android settings, but some features may not work without the relevant permission.

Health Connect and third-party health platforms

Just Run does not currently upload data to Health Connect, Fitbit, Google Fit, Samsung Health, or any developer-operated health platform. If a future version adds optional integration with a third-party health platform, this policy and the app's disclosures should be updated before that feature is released.

Data deletion

You can delete runs and app data from within the app where supported. You can also remove locally stored app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app from your phone and watch. Exported files, backups, GitHub posts, messages, or other data you shared outside the app must be deleted from those locations separately.

Data retention

Just Run keeps local app data until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Diagnostic logs may be overwritten or removed by the app, operating system, or your own cleanup actions.

Diagnostics and bug reports

Just Run can write local diagnostic logs and crash logs to help troubleshoot issues. These logs stay on your device unless you choose to retrieve or share them.

If you report a bug on GitHub, do not post sensitive personal data, precise home/work locations, private routes, or full diagnostic logs publicly unless you are comfortable making that information public. If private details are needed, ask for a safer contact method first.

Children

Just Run is not directed to children. The app is intended for general fitness use by people who can understand and manage the permissions, activity risks, and data described in this policy.

Security

Just Run relies on Android and Wear OS app storage, permissions, and device security controls to protect local data. No app or device storage can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure. Protect your phone, watch, exported files, and backups with appropriate device locks and account security.

Open source and modified versions

https://github.com/aaron-jencks/just_run

If someone modifies, forks, redistributes, or publishes their own version of Just Run, their version may have different data practices. Publishers of modified versions should provide their own accurate privacy policy and should not rely on this policy unless it accurately describes their app.

Changes to this policy
